# Changelog

## 1.2.1
- **A single voice no longer drifts across a cut-mode take.** In `cut` the audio head was pinned from the *previous* shot every time, so each shot imitated an imitation and by shot six the speaker was somebody else. The pin is now anchored to a FIXED tail: a named character to their own first shot (every shot of theirs, not only when the speaker changes), and an unnamed piece that uses one voice sentence throughout to shot 1's tail. Only the audio head moves - the picture seam is untouched - and `continue` mode, where the audio genuinely flows through the seam, is unchanged. A piece with two different voices and no names keeps the old behaviour, since anchoring two voices to one tail would be worse. `JOYLTX_VOICE_WALK=1` restores the previous behaviour for comparison.
- **Story Source shows the whole prompt store.** Three separate limits hid most of it: folders whose name begins with `_` were skipped as private (that is the naming convention of most prompt corpora - `_ARCHIVE`, `_REWRITES`, `_SURFACES` - and it hid 45 of 70 folders and 1226 of 1501 files in testing); the corpus offered folders only, so a single file could not be picked without copying it into `input/` first; and a depth-3 cap on `input/` hid 2831 of 2959 files. All three are gone. Corpus entries are tagged `corpus: <path>` so the dropdown stays readable, a root registered inside `input/` is no longer listed twice, and overlapping roots are de-duplicated. Existing saved canvases are unaffected - their input-relative and absolute paths still resolve.
- **Writer rules: faces stay in frame.** Identity re-locks visually from the reference photos and from the previous shot's closing frames, so a face the camera cannot see cannot be matched. Recurring characters now keep a readable face in frame and every shot ENDS on the face, settled - a head turn returns inside its own shot. The camera operator is exempt while filming (found footage has no face behind the lens) and re-locks on the shot where they step in front of it.
- **Writer rules: the strange thing never performs.** Anything uncanny is caught by the camera, not presenting to it: it never turns to the lens, never holds a pose, and the environment never reacts on cue.
- **Writer rules: the model's own camera vocabulary.** The writer had framing nouns but no motion names. It now uses the trained set (Push In, Truck Left, Static Shot, Shake Slightly, ...) with optional amplitude and speed, written as natural English rather than dropped-in labels. A held camera is "holds a static shot" - never "does not move", which freezes the whole frame.
- **Removed a negation the writer was told to emit.** Multi-character shots carried "they must never blend, merge, average or swap" into the prompt. There is no negative branch at cfg 1.0, so that sentence only put blending into the conditioning; the rule is now affirmative - state what each person distinctly HAS.
- **Bundled canvases reference models by bare filename** instead of the author's local subfolder layout.

## 1.2.0
- **Story Source panel** on the PLUS canvases: one node picks where a run's words come from - your scene idea, a file of story IDEAS walked one per queue by an INDEX primitive, or finished shot prompts that skip the writer. File pickers are dropdowns of everything under `ComfyUI/input`, folder entries walk a folder, and an optional `manual_path` reaches outside. Replaces the loose Prompts-from-File / two booleans / extra switch.
- **refs_attached** is wired from that panel: the writer then uses each character's own name and writes "looks exactly as in the reference photographs" instead of inventing a face - what Refs by Name needs to cast anyone.
- **Refs by Name**: `refs_root` is a dropdown of the character libraries found under `ComfyUI/input`; new `crop` (portrait / full photo) keeps the person and drops the room, so a scene photo no longer drags its set into the shot; `pick` defaults to one photo per character for the whole piece; `[ref: name]` in a shot forces a pick and is stripped before rendering.
- **Multishot sampler**: per-character identity and voice lock - from a character's second shot on, their own rendered face (face-area crop of their first shot) and their own audio tail are carried into every later shot of theirs, instead of the previous shot's.
- **The writer acts instead of narrating.** An event-driven premise (a creature comes alive, dogs burst in, a window breaks) used to come back as a seated character describing the whole story to camera in a locked chest-up frame, a line in every shot. Three stacked pressures caused it; the writer rules now carry an action discipline: every shot contains one physical event a muted viewer sees change, the subject performs with their body, a spoken line never narrates what the frame shows, and speech falls out of the moment - people exclaim, command and talk TO things, they do not narrate their own lives. In the seamless-take mode the locked frame now pulls events INTO frame instead of parking a commentator in front of them. Verified against the failing premise: 3 natural spoken beats of 12 shots, all events on camera.
- **JoyLTX Ref Image (manual)**: one loaded photo as the reference instead of the folder library - wire a Load Image into it and its outputs to the sampler's `ref_images`/`ref_mask`. Same portrait crop and sizing as Refs by Name; a batch gives one photo per shot; naming the character engages the identity + voice locks.
- **JoyLTX Retake**: redo one stretch of a finished clip and keep the rest. Load the rendered mp4, set a time window, write a prompt for that moment - everything outside the window is frozen as raw latents. Picture and sound are independent: redo both, keep the performance and change what the camera sees, or keep the picture and change the line. New `JoyLTX25_Retake_TEST` canvas. Run it with the SAME model and LoRAs the clip was rendered with.
- **Reference pointers only for people who have photographs.** The writer replaced EVERY named character's description with 'looks exactly as in the reference photographs' - including characters it invented, whose photographs do not exist. Now only characters the premise itself names get the pointer (and their stated age rides along); invented characters keep their written description, and Refs by Name strips any pointer whose subject has no photo folder, and says so in the report.
- **Cross-generation LoRA guard.** Applying an LTX-2.3 LoRA that carries audio-branch keys to an LTX-2.5 model garbles the voices (the video branches transplant; the audio path does not). The LoRA stack now warns loudly and names the fix: the _VIDEOONLY variant, or the LoRA built for the model's own generation.
- **A second speaker keeps their own voice.** The pinned audio head is what carries a voice across shots, and it was always the previous shot's - so in a take with two people, everybody ended up sounding like whoever spoke first. Now the pin follows who is speaking: same speaker as the last shot, the voice simply continues; a speaker heard earlier gets their own last tail back (in a seamless take the picture still continues from the previous shot, only the audio is swapped); a speaker heard for the first time gets no audio pin at all, so they arrive with their own voice. Needs Refs by Name's `ref_mask` to know who is in each shot - without it nothing changes. Note that a scene with two people belongs in `cut` mode: `continue` pins the picture, which is what makes it seamless, so a second speaker is painted into the first one's body and room no matter how hard their photo pulls.
- **Reference photographs now carry the face.** `ref_strength` defaults to 0.85 (was 0.5). Measured on one seed: at 0.5 a photo gives you the hair and the clothes and a stranger's face; 0.85-0.9 carries the face and the small things like glasses; 1.0 starts dragging the photograph's own room into the shot.
- **Age comes from the words, not the picture.** The bundled writer's reference rules now require the one age phrase from your premise in each identity sentence - the video model renders an adult otherwise, and a photograph of a teenager does not stop it. Everything else about how a person looks still stays out of the prose and comes from the pictures.
- Canvas notes for Story Source and Refs by Name; shorter node titles so nodes size to their lane.
